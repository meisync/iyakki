## The steps (each one a change that builds and passes the radio tests)

1. **Types (done, with a better shape than first written).** The agent depends on `iyakki-tile-api` (allowed: the rule only limits what tile-api depends on) and re-exports the types, so nothing is converted and there is one copy: `Station`, `CertPin`, `CertProblem` (with `pin` and `expiry`), `RadioState`, `BANDS` and the `Language` struct now live in `crates/iyakki-tile-api/src/radio.rs` (serde added to tile-api for the station file). The preset data (`LANGUAGES`) and the `RadioPort` trait stay where they were until step 5. The original text of the step follows for the record. Add `iyakki_tile_api::radio` with `Station`, `CertPin`, `CertProblem`, `RadioState`, `BANDS`, a `Language { name, stations }` and the `RadioPort` trait written in those types. The shell's `CxPort` converts to and from the agent's types (as `usage.rs` converts the agent's usage series) and hands the language list over through `RadioPort::languages()`, so the 547 lines of preset data stay in one place. The tile's old tests (they build `Station::new`, `StationStore`) switch to the api types or stay as adapter tests in the shell.
2. **The speaker question (done).** The tile holds `Arc<dyn SoundGate>` (from `RadioPort::sound()`; `Holder` and `SoundGate` are in `iyakki-tile-api::radio`) and a small `HoldAsk` of its own; the real gate (`RegistrySound` in `radio_port.rs`) owns the registry, the mixer switch and the stop thread, and the fake gate in `crates/iyakki-tile-radio/src/fake_tests.rs` has 3 new tests (keep, stop, a failing stop). The tile still draws the dialog through `soundask::render_question` (a shell function) and takes `answer_for_key` from `soundask`: both move to the toolkit in step 3. Audit lines are unchanged. The original text follows. Replace `Mode::Hold(Station, soundask::Ask)` with a plain state in the tile and three port methods: `holder() -> Option<Holder { says, kind }>` (what holds the sound card, or nothing), `stop_holder(&Holder) -> Receiver<Result<String, String>>` (the port does the killing, with its guards, on its own thread) and `hold_audit(action, holder, outcome)`. The dialog's drawing moves into the tile or `iyakki-uikit` (it only draws a question and three buttons). The registry and the mixer switch stay behind the port (`RadioPort::sound_conflict(&self) -> Option<Holder>`).
3. **Small shared things (done).** `FnKey` was already in `iyakki-uikit::keys` (the shell only re-exports it, so the tile now names the toolkit path); the answers and the dialog moved from `apps/soundask.rs` to `iyakki-uikit/src/askdialog.rs` (`Answer`, `answer_for_key`, `answer_for_tab`, the three button ids, `render_question`), re-exported by `soundask.rs` for the browser; `widgets`, `neon`, `blink`, `gfx`, `fx` and `ui` in the shell are all re-exports of the toolkit already. The original text follows. Move `FnKey` into `iyakki-uikit` (`keys.rs`) with a re-export in the shell so nothing else changes; check that `widgets::render_confirm` and the `fx` helpers come from the toolkit.
4. **The tile facade.** Add `pub radio: &'a dyn radio::RadioPort` to `TileCx` (one field, only because a tile needs it), give `iyakki_tile_api::App` default methods `media_key(&mut self, FnKey, &TileCx) -> bool` and `take_request(&mut self) -> Option<(String, String)>`, and make the other extracted tiles pass `&NoRadio`.
5. **The move.** `git mv` `radio.rs` to `crates/iyakki-tile-radio/src/lib.rs`, its fake-port tests with it, add the crate to the workspace and `dependency_ratchet`'s list of tile crates, write the shell adapter `apps/radio.rs` (about 80 lines, like `usage.rs`), keep the old shell tests that need real services as adapter tests, and remove `radio.rs` from `migrated_tiles.list` once the file is the adapter.

## Size

Steps 1 and 2 are the work: about 700 lines touched, 30 conversions, the 13 old tests and 7 fake-port tests adjusted. A day for one person who knows the code, with a compile of the shell crate after each step (3 to 10 minutes on a loaded machine). Steps 3 to 5 are half a day. Do them in this order; do not start 5 before 1 to 4 are merged.

## Why it was not done in the same round as the port

The data types and the speaker question cross three crates (the agent, the shell, the toolkit). A half-finished step 1 leaves two copies of every type with conversions between them and no benefit until step 5, so it is better done in one sitting than started and left. The same plan applies to MCP, MEDIA and SKILLS: their agent types (`ServerSpec`, `Tool`, `MediaStore`, `MediaIndex`, `DownloadQueue`, `Skill`, `Review`) are the step-1 work of those tiles (KI-984).
