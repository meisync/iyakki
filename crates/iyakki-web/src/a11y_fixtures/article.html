How tides work

The moon pulls on the sea. The pull is a little stronger on the side of the Earth that faces the moon, so the water gathers there. Read about the moon.

Two bulges

There is a second bulge on the far side. Together they make two high tides each day.

Spring and neap

  • Spring tides: sun and moon line up.
  • Neap tides: they pull at right angles.
